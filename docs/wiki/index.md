2D6 Companion is a free web app that sits beside your tabletop game. It holds the rules and tables you look up, rolls the dice for you, keeps your character sheet, draws the map as you explore, and writes a journal of your run. You still play the game; the app does the bookkeeping.

## Two games, one app

The app covers two games from the same family:

- **2D6 Dungeon** (with its Realm, Lairs and Haunted House extras): explore a dungeon room by room.
- **2D6 Void**: explore the Dark Hulk, a derelict ship, room by room.

You are always in one of them. The logo at the top left shows the game you are in, and a smaller logo of the other game sits just beside it. Click the small logo to switch. The app saves your choice and reloads into the other game. Each game keeps its own characters, saves and look, so switching never mixes them up.

## Unofficial, and the paid books are not included

2D6 Companion is a fan-made tool. It is not affiliated with DR Games or Toby Lancaster, who own 2D6 Dungeon and 2D6 Void. The app is free to use. The books are what make the games possible, so please buy them from [the DR Games online shop](https://drgames.co.uk/dr-games-online-shop/).

The app ships with the free material (the Dungeon core rules and the free Codex). Everything else comes from your own PDFs, which you open once in Settings and which stay on your device. See [Books and unlocking](settings-books.html).

## Where do I start?

1. New to the app? Read [Getting started](getting-started.html). It takes about five minutes.
2. Want to know what every button does? Read [Finding your way around](interface.html).
3. Ready to play 2D6 Dungeon? Go to [Start Game](dungeon-start-game.html) and then [Map](map.html).
4. Playing 2D6 Void? Unlock your book in [Books and unlocking](settings-books.html), then go to [Void Start Game](void-start-game.html).
5. Worried about losing your saves? See [Backups, tour and updates](settings-backups.html).
